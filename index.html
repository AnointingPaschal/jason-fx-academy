The Wick Theory

Bodies are retail.
Wicks are institutions.

The body shows where retail traders pushed price. The wick shows where bigger money stepped in — and left a clue.

STEP 01

Read the body

The body tells you the crowd's story. Useful — but not where the edge lives.

STEP 02

Follow the wick

A long wick is a rejection, a sweep, a footprint. Institutions cannot hide size.

STEP 03

Trade the clue

Wait for the clue, define your risk, then take the trade — or walk away.
