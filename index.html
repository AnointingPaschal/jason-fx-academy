The Wick Theory

Bodies are retail.
Wicks are institutions.

That is the whole idea, in one line. The body of a candle shows where retail traders pushed price. The wick shows where bigger money stepped in — and left a clue.

1. Read the body

The body tells you the crowd's story for that period. Useful — but it is not where the edge lives.

2. Follow the wick

A long wick is a rejection, a sweep, a footprint. Institutions cannot hide size. The wick is where they show it.

3. Trade the clue

Wait for the clue, define your risk, then take the trade — or walk away. All it takes is one trade, done properly.

The wicks leave clues.

In the film, the same pattern repeats across Forex, gold and indices: price sweeps a level, leaves a long wick, and then moves. Jason FX Academy teaches you to spot that sweep, confirm it, and manage the trade — instead of chasing price after it has already moved.

You will not learn a hundred indicators. You will learn one way of reading the market, and how to use it with patience.
